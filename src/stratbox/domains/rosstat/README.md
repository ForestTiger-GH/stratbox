# Rosstat statistical publications

The [domain catalog](domain.py) separates publication channels from reviewed
numerical series. [Common facts](common/) own publication rules and reusable
vocabularies; [series](series/) bind standard actions to [methods](methods/).

KEP2, population and regional industrial interpretation have explicit scientific
scopes. Other national or regional tables require their own reviewed mappings.
Publication discovery, physical decoding, numerical interpretation and products
retain separate evidence boundaries.

See [publication evidence](../../../../docs/current-how/rosstat-publications.md)
and [table interpretation](../../../../docs/current-how/rosstat-data-route.md).
