# Corporate-lending presentations

These product methods prepare and render regional debt, issuance, industry and
SORS results. Their content contracts preserve the selected period, portfolio,
territory and model qualifications.

The [domain catalog](../../domain.py) selects product specifications.
Source meaning belongs to [table methods](../../methods/tables/corporate_lending/);
reconstruction belongs to [calculations](../../calculations/). Common commands
own retention and publication of the encoded result.
