# Communication acts, protected analytical claims and repair across media

**Scientific question:** when Strategy Box converts a source-grounded analysis, forecast, validation or Work state into a chart, spreadsheet, AI message, note, notification or shared work item, what meaning must survive, and which downstream effects can actually be claimed? **Status:** bounded scientific interpretation of dated Strategy Box epistemic requirements, interface research, and specialist maintained communication evidence at a fixed review revision. No generic communication engine or collaboration delivery/acceptance policy is claimed to be installed.

## 1. Upstream correctness does not guarantee communicative correctness

An accurately parsed bank report can still be miscommunicated. Misleading summary, omitted definition, false color semantics, truncated chart axis, hidden bank/group perimeter, overstated causal inference or stale notification can cause the recipient to reasonably infer something the original data never supported.

This is a separate **transformation failure**: the upstream claim remains qualified and true as documented, while the recipient-facing projection changes what is safely understandable or relied on. Fluency, brevity, attractive colors and apparently authoritative wording do not by themselves establish communication quality.

A material communication Work should distinguish:
- the accepted upstream semantic object and its exact version;
- the communication **job**: inform, explain, ask, recommend, warn, instruct, approve, publish, correct or transfer responsibility;
- actor and communicative **force** (description, proposal, obligation, approval, commitment);
- recipients, presumed common ground, permitted reliance and foreseeable onward sharing;
- protected properties that must survive;
- representation/medium and controlled omissions;
- what actual delivery, understanding, agreement, acceptance or effect evidence exists.

These dimensions do not require a single universal Communication database. They require preservation where loss changes an interpretation or action.

## 2. Protected analytical properties

A transformation from Research/Result to Excel, dashboard, chat or PDF must preserve the **load-bearing properties** for its intended use:

| Source meaning | Typical dangerous transformation | Required restraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source vintage and reporting period | Shows only “latest” after new publication | Exact vintage/currentness or traceable reference |
| Bank, group and accounting perimeter | Drops the perimeter from a slide caption | Explicit comparison scope or known limitation |
| Official reported versus restored/estimated | Styles all values alike and hides evidence tier | Distinguishable qualification at relevant granularity |
| Hypothesis or conditional scenario | Summarizes “if H” as factual H | Preserve modality and conditions |
| Decomposition versus explanation | Calls a contribution “the cause” | Preserve causal status and alternative explanations |
| Forecast, target and stress | Uses shared chart legend “projection” | State future mode and uncertainty |
| Evaluation bound to criterion | Says simply “verified” | Criterion, scope, date and remaining checks |
| Recommendation versus Decision | AI message says “approved” | Separate authorized institutional transition |
| Job success versus artifact publication | Green chat card reports export ready before bytes commit | Verified Artifact state |
| Unknown effect | Notification says cancelled even after an unconfirmed external write | Preserve UNKNOWN and reconciliation action |
| Personal versus shared evidence | Group timeline exposes restricted technical logs | Audience/permission projection and redaction |
| Stale result with historical validity | Removes all warnings after rerender | Maintain currentness/reliance context |

A new table layout does not acquire authority over these upstream properties; it is a projection with a verification obligation.

## 3. Communication is action, but its words are not the effect

A message saying “approved” is not itself evidence of a valid approval. A displayed “we will update” statement is not automatically a binding commitment. A valid communication act may have institutionally recognized effects **only** under an explicit governing convention, actor authority, scope, and admitted state transition.

The product must distinguish:
**written → sent → published → delivered → read → understood → accepted → committed → acted on → effective**.
The exact steps vary by communication job; they are not a universal mandatory workflow. Delivery receipt proves neither comprehension nor agreement. A screenshot of a signed-looking button proves neither user Authorization nor actual artifact publication unless tied to authoritative event/effect evidence.

The Work/Thread timeline may show human text, AI proposals, real approvals and actual Run status near each other, but their canonical owners remain separate. An AI explanation of a completed calculation cannot change the Job/Artifact outcome or sign a Decision on behalf of the operator.

## 4. Recipient-relative sufficiency does not make truth relative

A dense specialist report and a concise executive slide may validly represent the same original result. The source truth does not change with audience. What changes is the **least sufficient explanation** and how quickly the reader can inspect protected qualifiers.

For a bank analysis, a board-facing number may need a perimeter/year/numerator/denominator qualifier and a clear caveat. A data-engineer view may need the full indicator ID, form code, source snapshot and transform. Providing a QR/link to a canonical manifest can satisfy some details if a later reader has access and the artifact will not reasonably circulate detached from its link.

Recipient groups differ in prior knowledge, authorization and intended action. One representation may serve them safely; otherwise produce distinct **synchronized projections** over a common source. Sending a personal technical exception to an entire shared node is neither necessary clarity nor valid authorization.

## 5. Compression, repeated summaries and fragment survival

Each summarization, translation, visualization or AI transformation may lose context. Repeatedly summarizing a summary compounds loss even when each sentence reads well. The source of a second-order summary is another projection, not an independent corroborating witness. Critical claims must route back to an authoritative source version before a stronger reliance is asserted.

For durable slides, chat excerpts, screenshots, charts and fragments shared separately, assess reasonably foreseeable standalone use. A graph comparing bank profitability can become misleading when separated from the note specifying IFRS versus RAS and group perimeter. A material fragment should preserve or robustly route those qualifications; this does **not** demand every sentence contain a complete bibliography.

Omission dispositions include safe/common-ground assumption, reference/routing to source, constraint enforced elsewhere, intentionally immaterial for the named job, or **UNRESOLVED** requiring stop/clarification. Silence about a high-consequence limitation is not neutral minimalism.

## 6. Representation selection is an engineering choice

| User task | Candidate representation | Counterargument |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scan current Run failures | Scoped status table with exact problem links | Raw log first increases noise and can leak technical data |
| Inspect long-run causal chain | Timeline + stage/effect transitions | Timeline alone can obscure shared resource conflicts |
| Compare banks over periods | Table or chart with explicit measure/perimeter | Chart axis and aggregation can fabricate visual difference |
| Reconcile source vintages | Version matrix, side-by-side evidence and declared bridge | One “latest” document erases ex-ante history |
| Show branch dependencies | DAG/graph when true branching exists | A linear four-step execution reads more clearly as numbered stages |
| Explain uncertainty | Bounded text + evidence links + qualified interval | Decorative confidence badge collapses distinct unknowns |
| Decide on destructive cleanup | Exact target/effect plan and evidence of approval | One generic confirmation cannot show actual blast radius |

Color, hierarchy, sorting, typography, title and notification timing can change a recipient's practical interpretation even when the raw numerical cell remains identical. Accessibility (screen readers, focus, contrast, keyboard alternatives) is part of meaning transmission, not superficial polish.

## 7. Currentness, correction and repair

A finished communication can remain historically correct while becoming stale for current action. Distinguish updated, corrected, superseded, withdrawn, expired and historically retained views. **Updating** a forecast after new information is not necessarily **correcting** an error in the original. A message deleted after a wrong instruction does not reverse any later effect.

When a material representation defect is found:
1. identify the erroneous projection and exact upstream source;
2. determine affected artifacts, readers, accepted Decisions and external actions where possible;
3. issue an appropriately scoped correction/withdrawal or successor with identifiable provenance;
4. notify accountable recipients if earlier reliance may have been unsafe;
5. retain historical cause, what changed and evidence of new comprehension/acceptance where required.

A visible red banner does not automatically set urgent priority; the responsible owner and actual response policy determine whether a new obligation arises.

## 8. Falsification cases and explicit alternatives

- A summary of uncertain reconstructed SORS data says “official figure”: **fail** even if Excel byte output is valid.
- A chart changes the vertical axis to exaggerate a 0.2 percentage-point margin shift: test what reasonable analysts infer, not only arithmetic values.
- A model turns “analysis recommends” into “management decided”: **fail** without actual Decision Authority.
- A human says “noted” in chat, and application marks review accepted: delivery/reading ≠ acceptance.
- A private Artifact appears in another user's Thread due to stale cached permissions: access-specific projection **fail**.
- A new official vintage revises a series but an old chart keeps green “current” styling: version/reliance **fail**.
- A correction is sent to the original author only although a team has already used the wrong report: propagation scope needs investigation.
- Two audience versions disagree because the shorter slide uses a different source snapshot: cross-projection consistency **fail** unless intended.
- A fully source-faithful report is too complicated for the recipient to use correctly: fidelity may pass while communication usability fails.
- A very concise slide omits no protected properties but adds unnecessary repeated boilerplate: test end-to-end cost and reader clarity; shorter is not always better.

**Decision frontier:** what receives durable communication identity, which delivery/understanding/acceptance receipts the product will support, correction propagation rules, recipient permissions and the quality evidence required for different banking decision consequences. The research argues for qualified projection semantics, not a universal chat platform or automatic organizational authority.
