# Forecast vintages, conditional futures and the decision boundary

**Scientific question:** how should an analytical bank/macro product preserve the meaning of a forecast under its original information state, distinguish forecasts from scenarios, plans and stress paths, and prevent subsequent evaluation or reporting from granting it new authority? **Status:** bounded scientific synthesis grounded in the dated Strategy Box epistemic original and semantic decomposition original, with direct challenge against specialist forecasting and decision-science maintained sources at their fixed versions. **No dedicated Strategy Box Forecast issuance engine or decision-governance subsystem is claimed to exist.**

## 1. A historically situated assertion, not any future-looking spreadsheet cell

A material Forecast concerns a target property not yet established for a specific information state. Meaning requires target and bank/reporting perimeter, target horizon, conditions, historical information cutoff, uncertainty mode, supporting evidence and how later observations can resolve the claim. A nowcast may be about a present but not-yet-reported quantity: clock time alone does not determine forecast identity.

| Form | What is primarily asserted | Mistaken promotion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forecast | Qualified assertion about an unknown target under a historical information state | Model output becomes a future fact or authorized target |
| Conditional scenario | Consequences **if** a premise H holds | The system says H is likely or true |
| Stress path | Deliberately adverse/diagnostic conditional world | Severe condition described as baseline prediction |
| Baseline projection | Path under specified modeling convention | Convention treated as unconditional world truth |
| Counterfactual | Consequence under action/intervention assumptions | Model counterfactual treated as measured causal effect |
| Target | Desired or required condition | Target treated as probability distribution |
| Plan | Intended resource/action sequence | Planned future treated as predictive accuracy |
| Strategy | Persistent coordination of coupled choices | One scalar projection treated as a complete strategy |
| Decision | Authorized resolution of open choice | AI recommendation represented as management authorization |
| Signpost/warning | Observable condition relevant to a response policy | A warning automatically changes the commitment |

A report may legitimately contain all these types. Their authority follows semantic identity and qualified relations, not visual layout.

## 2. Candidate, issuance and immutable historical vintage

A **Forecast Candidate** is a proposed, inspectable assertion. **Issued Forecast Vintage** is the historically accepted/issued forecast identity under the applicable authority. Once issued, the original assertion, target, distributions, date, source/model versions, conditions and ex-ante information position must remain recoverable. Later changed source data or improved models produce a new successor, correction, withdrawal or reissue relation; silently overwriting the sole 2024 workbook with new 2026 numbers destroys historical validity.

A source revision can make an old forecast obsolete for current reliance but cannot make the issued old forecast disappear as a historically recorded object. Comparing versions requires decomposing changes due to source revision, model revision, changed target/perimeter, assumptions or different intended use. A later issue with better accuracy does not prove the old method was irresponsible.

## 3. Ex-ante integrity goes beyond the dated input table

Historical forecasting evidence may require exact source publication vintages, accessible documents, registry/classifier versions, training/data cutoffs, model code/weights, retrieved context and dated human knowledge. Asking an AI to “pretend it is 2024” does not stop a pretrained model from using events it learned in 2025. Such an exercise can be a qualified hindsight simulation, but not a clean ex-ante backtest without controlled information boundaries.

For bank comparisons this includes: institution versus group, IFRS versus RAS, cumulative versus standalone profit, revised source series, changed classification and perimeter. A time label like 2024 is not sufficient evidence that the value was known in 2024.

## 4. Uncertainty and claim force are not one number

A forecast may be a point, quantile, probability distribution, interval, ensemble, category, path, rank, hazard, time-to-event or qualitative assertion. Forecast probability is distinct from epistemic support confidence, model uncertainty, source ambiguity, data missingness and applicability. A universal confidence score cannot replace these differences. One should sometimes decline a precise numerical forecast and produce a conditional scenario, monitoring Work, coarse range or explicit abstention.

A historical SORS latent interval is an **identified historical amount range**, not a future prediction interval. Identical spreadsheet notation does not make them interchangeable.

## 5. Resolution, evaluation, skill and use are separately established

Resolution matches an issued forecast to later observed target outcomes, with exact measurement and event adjudication. Evaluation measures declared quality under an applicable score and population. Forecast skill compares with a benchmark; **decision utility** depends on loss, values, resource constraints and actual user risk policy. Neither accuracy nor skill grants authority to adopt a plan.

- A point, probabilistic event, interval and stress path demand different forms of evaluation; a stress scenario is not a failed forecast merely because the adverse condition did not happen.
- Lucky one-off accuracy does not establish a reliable forecasting process; a well-formed forecast can be wrong.
- A score over reporting survivors or available labels may not generalize to the original bank population. Selection, revision and hindsight effects must be disclosed.
- Agreement among forecasts based on the same source/method cannot count as independent evidence; the relevant lineage matters.

## 6. Forecast versus Decision and adaptive Strategy

The valid chain is: evidence and observations → Forecast/Analysis → recommendation under objectives/values/constraints → authorized Decision → commitment → execution/effects → observed outcome and review. No step silently entails the next.

A Decision may choose action, defer, investigate, stage, preserve an option, delegate, prohibit, terminate or amend a rule. A strategy is worth durable identity only when coupled decisions across capital/liquidity, scarce capacity, path dependence, response-contingent actors and preserved options require coherent long-term coordination. Intended, enacted and realized strategy can diverge. Ex-post bank performance is neither proof nor refutation of the original ex-ante decision quality without context.

When probability models are unreliable, robust action across several unresolved hypotheses can be responsible. It does not prove any one hypothesis true. Competing methods include expected utility, multicriteria tradeoffs, real options, minimax regret, adaptive paths, games and bounded emergency choices. Robustness can be overly conservative; option preservation has costs; “get more data first” can delay a reversible time-sensitive action.

## 7. Representation must retain conditions and historical identities

Excel sheet, prose memo, chart, PDF, AI answer and warning notification may represent the same forecast under different consumer tasks. They must preserve material target, perimeter, vintage, conditionality, uncertainty and legitimacy of reliance. A green “validated” label could certify schema consistency only; it is not a skill assessment or a management Decision.

A new forecast from a weighted combination is a new Forecast Candidate if it makes a new predictive claim, not just a presentation of prior forecasts. A comparison chart is not necessarily a new forecast; it can be a derived view. Do not accidentally assign new semantic identity because a visualization looks novel.

## 8. Falsifying probes

1. Present a conditional stress path to an assistant; the output must retain its **if** clause.
2. Rebuild a 2024 forecast from revisions first published in 2026; tag hindsight contamination instead of ex-ante confidence.
3. Retain a 2024 issued vintage when a 2026 revision supersedes it; the old historical claim still exists.
4. Evaluate stress path and probabilistic bank profit forecast with appropriate different criteria; reject a universal accuracy score.
5. Publish only famous “successful” historical forecasts; the archive should not imply representative forecast skill.
6. Change bank/group perimeter after issuance; preserve the original target and disclose bridges for comparisons.
7. Make an AI summary say “approve”; keep it as proposal unless actual authorized Decision exists.
8. Trigger source monitoring with no accountable response owner; show observation, not “adaptive Strategy completed”.
9. Aggregate forecasts whose evidence originates from the same publisher; do not count derivative repetition as independent confirmation.
10. Create a convenient styled workbook with hidden uncertainty columns; scientific result must remain recoverable without relying on styling.

**Current evidence and limits:** the existing Python core supports source and analytical transformations and domain-specific evidence models; a full Forecast Candidate/Vintage issuance, scoring, Decision authority or strategy monitoring runtime has not been demonstrated in Strategy Box. **Open Product choices:** initial forecasting domain, historical retention, issuer/acceptance role, evaluation regimes, source and model validity, external AI inputs and user-facing qualification depth. This Science article is an epistemic specification candidate, not implementation acceptance.
