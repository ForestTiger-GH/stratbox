# Corporate lending and industry calculations

Corporate source series preserve their distinct regional, district, national, industry and SME layouts. Numerical interpretation retains published categories, source totals, units, date anchors and missingness. A nominal source match cannot override conflicting actual evidence.

Industry debt and issuance have different period meaning. Debt represents the declared stock date; issuance uses the declared reference interval. APK/LPK calculations are explicit formula commands over comparable operands. They do not alter the primary publisher observations or fill missing components silently.

SORS is a coupled restoration operation with independently callable source preparation, constraints, propagation, inheritance, reconciliation, feasibility, optimization, certification and assembly commands. Global feasibility remains a prerequisite for certified values. It differs from independent calculation over each time series.
