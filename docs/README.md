## Meaning of evidence and admission

**Observed** means the identified repository revision actually contains a particular implementation or test. It is not proof that an application was installed, executed successfully or behaves identically in all environments.

**Research-supported** means an engineering statement has a stated scope, original bases, trade-offs and falsification conditions. It may be useful and internally coherent without being product-approved or independently certified.

**CANDIDATE / CONDITIONAL** means a proposed WHAT/HOW/Architecture option still requires the appropriate Product owner, scope, deployment and acceptance criteria. A documented candidate does not become a feature commitment.

A completed step, produced Excel file, accepted Result, published Artifact, authoritative Decision and verified external effect are not interchangeable. The maintained Science explains those distinctions and preserves material negatives as well as positive proposals.

## Repository and platform boundary

The public [stratbox core](https://github.com/ForestTiger-GH/stratbox) owns subject-area bank/macro statistical processing and its neutral contracts; [stratbox-windows](https://github.com/ForestTiger-GH/stratbox-windows) owns its currently observed Windows implementation. The external managed platform is a separate product responsible only for the lifecycle and interfaces actually established by its own versioned contracts. Conceptual headless, Web, Android, agent and remote-execution architectures remain future research/design options, not evidence of deployed components.

The pinned analytical and desktop code studies are static reconstructions; **this publication does not assert a successful GUI, full test suite, real deployment, concurrency, fault injection, security acceptance or cross-device compatibility run.** It contains no deployment-specific confidential implementation materials; extension boundaries are described neutrally.

## Why the research archive remains available

The maintained [Science index](science/README.md) is the proper entry for bounded thematic knowledge. It must eventually allow a reader to understand every material supported conclusion, counterargument and limitation without reading its historical Research first. The historical [docs_old](../docs_old/README.md) stays available for forensic provenance, not as a second current owner of WHAT/HOW.

The [frozen source census and conservation workflow](../_mw/epochs-001-strategy-box-development/consolidation/README.md) retain material source identities, provisional source-located candidates, unresolved contradictions and verification findings. **No published file count, working link or saved original proves complete semantic conservation.** Full Research Amputation and independent source↔Science evidence verification remain mandatory before the word “lossless” may describe the entire product.
