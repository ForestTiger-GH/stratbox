# Strategy Box — full lossless Science consolidation program

**Applicable to:** the existing active consolidation Commission, expanded by the 2026-10-11 late Research closure. **Primary outcome:** a self-contained, complete, public-safe `docs/science/` body that preserves **all materially significant admissible Research meaning**, not merely selected interesting articles. Complementary Current HOW, WHAT, HOW, Architecture, selective LDD (Low-Level Design) and scope-activated LCD (Low Code Design) retain separate truth, authority and assurance.

## 1. Preservation meaning

**Lossless is semantic, not verbatim.** Preserve everything whose omission can change the correct understanding, argument, future design, verification, operation, applicability, interpretation of evidence, or rational choice among alternatives:

- main and subsidiary conclusions, supporting mechanisms, explicit assumptions, scope and maturity;
- definitions and distinctions, algorithms/formulae/limits, important examples and negative test cases;
- unsuccessful proposals, counterarguments, contrary evidence, superseded historic positions **with reasons**, residual conflicts and UNKNOWNs;
- source authority, exact revisions, independence/dependence, time, evidence quality, confidence and reopen triggers;
- substantive original developer needs and explicitly competing implementation suggestions, distinguishing an instruction from an unapproved proposal.

Do **not** treat repetition, formatting, boilerplate or a derivative paragraph count as material meaning. Deduplicate only with exact source-to-destination proof, while preserving competing variants and temporal shifts. A large Research manuscript may map to multiple topical Science modules; one generic paragraph is not a valid substitute for detailed content.

**Research findings have Science destinations even when they are rejected or conditional.** Rejection, contradiction and obsolescence are themselves valuable scientific knowledge when their rationale matters. Product commitments simultaneously route to WHAT through the appropriate human/decision authority; direct source-code facts route to Current HOW, with their Science implications derived separately. Do not pretend every source-code line is a scientific hypothesis.

## 2. Mandatory source-to-meaning accounting

Start from `sources/CENSUS.csv`, the additive [corpus closure](sources/INPUT-CLOSURE-2026-10-11.md), the exact supplementary human source carriers when available and all relevant original 01/02/03/04 Research. Rehydrate prior legitimate 19 full-read and 582 provisional material units; **check rather than recopy them**.

For each substantial original perform:

1. **Full primary read:** exact file/blob and section/line locators, not only summary, table of contents, later synthesis or search snippet.
2. **Fine-grained extraction:** one or more addressable units per distinct material claim, mechanism, caveat, comparison, example, assumption, negative result, alternative, evidence role or decision-relevant qualifier.
3. **Status and authority:** `CURRENT_CODE_OBSERVED`, `RESEARCH_SUPPORTED`, `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`, `HUMAN_INTENT`, `HISTORICAL`, `DISPUTED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `UNKNOWN` or a precisely defined compatible scheme.
4. **Cross-original reconciliation:** equivalent, derivative, dependent, conflicting, refinements and version changes. Neither multiple similar studies nor a later synthesis count as independent corroboration.
5. **Canonical final disposition:** Science owner file + exact anchor/claim, and if applicable Current HOW/WHAT route. Record why apparent duplicates/irrelevant source fragments need no second body of text.
6. **Independent reverse challenge:** Science→grounds and Source→material meaning; verify source completeness separately from the publication's literary quality.

Useful unit record fields: `unit_id`, `source_id`, `source_rev`, `source_span`, `material_meaning`, `kind/status`, `evidence/use`, `dependency/conflict`, `science_destination`, `other_owner_route`, `conservation_proof`, `independent_review`. A source-level `verified` flag requires all its material units assigned to checkable destinations with no silent residue.

## 3. Scientific modules must follow real problem spaces

Do **not** mirror research numbers, runs, timestamps or manuscript filenames as the main Science architecture. Build and refine a normalized topical map, including at least:

1. Statistical data/source identity, published versions, provenance, formats, reference registries, domain calculations and methodological validity.
2. Reusable analytical capabilities, operation/command/scenario/cascade/scheme models; direct Python/notebook consumption and machine-call semantics.
3. User work, planning, execution, background work, concurrency, cancellation, retry, effects, acceptance and recovery.
4. State, persistence, sessions/chat history, artifact lifecycle, storage, file formats and source/data workspaces.
5. Multiuser collaboration, identities, scoped permissions, visibility and trust/security assumptions.
6. Local/host/client deployment; Windows/Web/Android reuse and interface/visual/settings/customization issues.
7. External integration boundaries, platform maturity/contracts and bounded AI/cognitive integration without publishing other systems' internals.
8. Errors, observability, diagnostics, health, support, user-visible status and evidence quality.
9. Assurance, failure models, maintenance, architecture options, complexity/performance/resource trade-offs and decisions still needing evidence.
10. Consolidated whole-system conceptual model, taxonomy, contradictions, negative proposals and open research questions.

Include material niche/subdomain findings instead of flattening the corpus into only ten overview articles. This list is a starting issue map, **not a binding folder taxonomy**.

## 4. Protected/public boundary

The open `stratbox` and `stratbox-windows` repositories must never contain identifying specifics, internal implementation details or protected source extracts from access-restricted extensions. No confidential source file names, identity hints or provenance locators are imported into open ledgers. Preserve all **public-safe scientific generalizations** with their qualification, while keeping confidential details and protected source accounting exclusively with the authorized private owner. An access-protected original outside the public corpus cannot be claimed fully publicly assimilated. Maintain the precise separation between **lossless admitted/public-safe meaning** and **protected unpublishable matter**.

Methodological/cognitive-project technical paths and exact external Work instruction links remain in permitted working method routes only; published Science is Strategy Box-specific and self-contained, while acknowledging relevant conceptual models without exposing external internal architecture.

## 5. Execution order and rehydration

Keep one continuous commissioned work program with **bounded, verifiable checkpoints**, not a false micro-waterfall or repeated premature global closure audits:

- Freeze/admit all Research generations 01–04, new human signals and direct evidence revisions; define the true denominator.
- Finish full source reading and material-unit extraction; do not overstate completeness from line-range allocation alone.
- Reconcile within themes and across original generations, especially 03 synthesis vs 04 revisions and user's actual comments.
- Build complete Science in independent topical modules, progressively verifying material coverage.
- Repair Current HOW from direct implementation facts and update WHAT/HOW/Architecture/LDD and proportionate LCD where **authorized and source-supported**, without converting Research into accepted commitments.
- Perform source-first, output-backward, cross-topic, cold-reader, machine consistency, restricted-content and independent-assurance checks; close only after all gates pass.
- Update `STATE.md` and the affected per-source/owner ledgers after each meaningful batch. Persist exact next action and revisions *before* context compaction or a real session reset, then rehydrate from durable owners and direct sources. Logical reset is always mandatory; physical reset only when required and available.

Avoid spending most of the work budget re-auditing links, statistics and the same early closed samples while the corpus remains overwhelmingly unread. Give progress indicators for each stage: indexed / fully read / located units / Science-assimilated / reverse-checked / independently checked, not just a count of commits or Markdown files.

## 6. Strict completion gates

**No global `LOSSLESS_PASS` until:**

1. All admitted source identities are accounted, including late 04 Research and separately bound human inputs; exclusions are explicit and justified, with no public restricted-material leakage.
2. Every *material Research unit* has an exact, stable, independently readable Science location retaining meaning, status, assumptions, counterexamples, competing models and proof limitations. Any deduplicated/obsolete unit has an explicit equivalent or historically faithful Science representation.
3. All file-source states have justified terminal disposition; `unaccounted` = **0** for the admitted scope. `not_full_read` = **0** for admitted substantial Research originals; non-Research files receive an appropriate evidence/auxiliary disposition.
4. All accepted user requirements and unresolved product alternatives have identified rightful WHAT/Decision routes, without falsely claiming unapproved APIs or architectures.
5. All substantially relevant Science claims can be traced back to exact supported sources, and all meaningful original Research findings can be found in Science **without reading Research history**.
6. Cross-theme terminology, baselines, open conflicts, dependent evidence and negative findings have survived independent challenge. Separate cold readers can resolve representative ordinary, difficult and conflicting questions from the published corpus.
7. Public safety/security and publication identity/link checks are completed against the exact final tree; structural token scans alone are insufficient.
8. Distinct competent verification has actually been performed where claimed. Single-agent self-review is a producer check, never a substitute for independently passed assurance.
9. Final dated closure report gives exact denominator, source/unit counts, zero material residue, precise `docs/science/` owners and remaining **product choices** separately from scientific completeness.

If an input is unavailable, conflicts cannot be represented safely, or a required independent check cannot be conducted, report precise **`PARTIAL / BLOCKED`** at the corresponding scope. Never reinterpret a short summary, saved source blob, plan or more research as equivalent to full semantic conservation.
