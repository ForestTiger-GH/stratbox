## Science → original: selected disconfirmation probes

1. A statement “the external platform currently runs Strategy Box's authorized hosted Jobs” is **not supported** by the Research or direct Current HOW. Early Research **proposed** it. Pinned public AppDock process-launch code permits ordinary background spawn but explicitly reports `BACKEND_UNAVAILABLE` for `host_delegated`.
2. A Science claim that “the user has accepted a host” is **now supported by a later direct Product Decision**, **not** by the original Research paper. The old host ownership recommendation cannot replace the accepted Product owner.
3. The source-proposed `catch_up_latest` is appropriate for some refresh snapshots but may lose legally or analytically significant skipped periods. It must not become a universal default.
4. `WARN` data validation does not automatically imply a valid report. A release might support qualified publication under a separately admitted policy; missing policy requires a safe halt/review rather than silently upgrading WARN to PASS.
5. Rejected proposal (“MCP and machine-first API is the domain's source of truth”) remains a useful explicit negative boundary: the common curated domain capability remains independent of AI or a transport projection.
