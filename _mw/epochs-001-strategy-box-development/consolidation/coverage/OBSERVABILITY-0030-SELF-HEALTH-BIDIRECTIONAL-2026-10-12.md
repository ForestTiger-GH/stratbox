## Reverse Science-to-source and counterarguments

- A “Problem was registered” claim requires authentic recorder acceptance or resolvable receipt; a failed network call permits `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`, not automatic `NOT_WRITTEN` or a fabricated reference. The first state is **derived challenge** to the source's shorthand §36, grounded in asynchronous lost-response ambiguity.
- A `FAILURE` of the domain does not automatically invalidate a successful record of that failure; equally, a logger failure cannot automatically turn a valid XLSX into a failed computation.
- Fail-closed **mandatory** audit applies only where an admitted authorization/effect policy makes the audit a precondition. Universal denial of all pure reads during optional logging failure would be a bad generalization.
- A selected physical `system/logs/` or SQLite layout, fixed fourteen/thirty-day retention, platform-hosted Problem SDK and guaranteed self-crash recording remain **Research implementation suggestions**, not Product Decisions.
- Backend and privacy limitations follow actual direct source code/contract checks; citing the old Research's “already implemented” platform concepts alone cannot certify deployed services.
