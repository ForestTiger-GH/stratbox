The linked [CI role audit](../tools/audit_nonresearch_source_roles.py) verifies count, distinct IDs, role-to-owner candidate coverage and that all 566 remain `unaccounted`; it does **not** read source bodies, execute all tests, attest binaries, certify owner competence, or change the census.

**Why this matters:** 402 source records were still marked `not_full_read`. Some are code, images, bytecode or generated local state for which a prose-to-Science transfer is nonsensical; others are actual code/legacy documents requiring direct review and explicit material contribution to Current HOW/Science. A role category is a **routing hint**; it is never enough to upgrade `material_unit_state=verified`. Distinct final dispositions require individual or appropriately batched direct evidence and a separate independent challenge where the Commission demands it.

**Safety:** generated historical settings and state files should be treated as potentially user-specific and evaluated by metadata/appropriate restricted handling. Avoid displaying their raw bytes in public documentation or research reports. Content classification and publication privilege remain open.

**Next obligation:** prioritize direct executable critical paths and critical integration manifests/tests; review historical legacy documents for material meaning before any exclusion.
