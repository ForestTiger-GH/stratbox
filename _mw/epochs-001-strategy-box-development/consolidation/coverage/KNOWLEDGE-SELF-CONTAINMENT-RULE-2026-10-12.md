# Knowledge independence of removable historical Research

**Owner direction (2026-10-12):** the maintained `docs/` Knowledge Product must be self-contained: its scientific and engineering conclusions, counterarguments, source-evidence grades, negative tests and open decisions must remain usable even if the original Research folder is deleted as a single subsequent action. Do not create new links, instructions to open, or navigation requirements to `_mw/`, `research/` or `docs_old/` from any document under `docs/`.

**Separate responsibilities:** `_mw/consolidation/` continues to own archival source hashes, exact line spans, provenance ledgers, source-to-Science transfer checks, author processing and independent assurance. Its Work reports may link **toward** maintained Science. Science and all other `docs/` sections must contain the substantive meaning in situ; direct references to current pinned **implementation code** and genuine independent external standards/literature remain permitted. Identifiers of older source positions may remain as **non-navigational evidence annotations** where they are helpful to audit; they cannot be required to understand a claim or call an unavailable source.

**One-time migration:** `tools/migrate_knowledge_research_links.py --apply` removes only Markdown links with destinations under removable `_mw`, original Research and `docs_old`; it keeps visible statement text, old source-position geometry as non-hyperlinked metadata when needed by structural audit, and legitimate code links. The parallel CI guard rejects new links to those paths. This does **not** prove semantic losslessness: source-first and reverse consumer tests and a distinct competent verifier remain obligatory. A deleted Research original with no published substantive destination is still material loss, even if no dead URL survives.

**Loss of original DOCX:** the user has classified the original DOCX carrier as LOST. Recovered textual witness is lower-attestation knowledge, and exact original binary claims are excluded; this never authorizes false attribution or removes the need to conserve the accessible Research corpus.
