# Strategy Box — Knowledge Product Architecture Contract

**Status:** PREPARATORY CONTRACT / to be revalidated and bound by the full-cycle Commission before substantive assembly.
**Purpose:** produce one independently readable, public-safe and lossless-accounted knowledge product for Strategy Box without copying Research History into the publication surface.

## Distinct maintained semantic roles

| Published owner | Meaning | Independence test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `docs/science/` | Qualified current scientific understanding, explanations, counterarguments, limitations, alternatives, source-use | Ordinary study/challenge no longer needs historical Research |
| `docs/what/` | One target Product definition with component/variant profiles and authorized commitments | Product expectations clear without Science/HOW |
| `docs/current-how/` | Observed mechanisms and direct evidence at exact code and environment revisions | No future design represented as present fact |
| `docs/how/` | Target realization, application, operation, recovery and verification mechanisms | Implementation/operation can proceed without historical Research |
| `docs/architecture/` | Independent allocation of responsibility across domain, app runtime, surfaces and external boundaries | Coordination/owner review without copying HOW |
| `docs/ldd/` | Selective local technical specification for risky and complex mechanisms | Testable local detail, not a second code owner |

`docs_old/` is a non-current source archive. `_mw/` owns Work, research, lineage, source census, unit dispositions, reconciliation, decisions and proof gates. Published indexes/complete editions are generated projections, not competing semantic owners. No publication manifest is declared before actual materialized, verified product exists.

## Scientific assembly contract

Work across all admitted material units: statement and force, current/historical/hypothetical class, source revision/span, evidence nature and dependence, assumptions, limitations, alternative/negative positions, conflicts, applicability, implications and reopen condition. Organize by emergent problem spaces; retain source-to-destination and destination-to-basis coverage. A copied paragraph is not evidence of assimilated knowledge. Preserve critical source semantics without exposing restricted material.

Science is about the engineering understanding of Strategy Box; runtime bank and macro data remain in the product's own data ownership.

## Target admission contract

Product commitments come from a known Human/Decision Authority and explicit obligations. Research recommendations may challenge or support them but cannot turn into accepted target commitments by mere similarity. WHAT must contain both abstract and concrete target representations. HOW mechanisms and architecture shall trace those obligations; unresolved physical placement decisions remain conditional and explicitly owned.

## External boundaries

`stratbox` is an independent headless analytic core. `stratbox-windows` owns its current Windows implementation. The logical application authority and future platform surfaces require product-level semantic coordination; no separate repository is assumed without an actual design decision. AppDock is an external platform owner: its connector, future Data Plane and Control Plane are version/feature-dependent, never invented as already supported. Allowed cognitive and automation clients are neutral permissioned capability consumers.

## Assurance, publication and reopen conditions

Completion requires exact-source census, unit-level disposition, direct evidence use, inter-theme reconciliation, supported Science without Research History, complete Product Commitment census and authorized WHAT, WHAT-to-HOW trace, design-freedom/failure/recovery coverage, external/public boundary checks, usable cold-reader paths, release link/integrity checks, and a reproducible publication identity manifest. Close only the explicitly declared source universe and consumer tasks.

Revise affected owner slices when new code, evidence, Product Decisions, external contracts or observed execution invalidate their premises. Keep existing accepted claims unaffected unless evidence of material impact requires reopening.
